4-Harvest-Cycle Conversion Pipeline

Farmers register Feb–May and complete 4 harvest cycles for full organic certification.

0

Year 0: Intake / Wildlife Friendly (WF)

Initial registration of ~500 new farmers annually (Jan–May), audit in Sept, first harvest in Nov. Paid 200 KHR per kg ($0.0488/kg) on 40% of total production.

1

Transition Year 1 (Ibis I)

Second in-conversion harvest cycle. Paid 200 KHR per kg ($0.0488/kg) conservation incentive on 40% of total farm production.

2

Transition Year 2 (Ibis II)

Third in-conversion harvest cycle. Paid 250 KHR per kg ($0.0610/kg) conservation incentive on 40% of total farm production.

3+

Cycle 4 (Year 3+): Fully Organic (Selling Paddy)

Full 36-month certification achieved. Eligible to sell organic paddy to IRCC at guaranteed premium purchase prices.
