Historical Variety Mix Evolution (2022–2026)

Procurement Shares

The commercial evolution of 4-variety organic purchases over the past 5 seasons:

Phka Rumduol (Fragrant Jasmine): 87.1% (2022) → 46.5% (2026)

Traditionally the dominant crop; adjusted as farmers diversify to meet regional market demand.

White Rice: 8.7% (2022) → 40.2% (2026)

Surged rapidly; essential for IRCC to contract white rice to prevent non-organic sales leakage.

Red Jasmine: 0.8% (2022) → 11.8% (2026)

High-margin niche variety with excellent resilience in sandy soils.

Seed Paddy: 3.3% (2022) → 1.4% (2026)

Consistently maintained at ~1.4%–3.3% to ensure certified pure seed stocks.

4-Harvest-Cycle Conversion Pipeline

Farmers register Feb–May and complete 4 harvest cycles for full organic certification.

0

Year 0: Intake / Wildlife Friendly (WF)

Initial registration of ~500 new farmers annually (Jan–May), audit in Sept, first harvest in Nov. Paid 200 KHR per kg ($0.0488/kg) on 40% of eligible production.

1

Transition Year 1 (Ibis I)

Second in-conversion harvest cycle. Paid 200 KHR per kg ($0.0488/kg) conservation incentive on 40% of eligible farm production.

2

Transition Year 2 (Ibis II)

Third in-conversion harvest cycle. Paid 250 KHR per kg ($0.0610/kg) conservation incentive on 40% of eligible farm production.

3+

Cycle 4 (Year 3+): Fully Organic (Selling Paddy)

Full 36-month certification achieved. Eligible to sell organic paddy to IRCC at guaranteed premium purchase prices.
